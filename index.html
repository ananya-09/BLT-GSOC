Google Summer of Code

Google Summer of Code (GSOC)
with
Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP) Foundation

The Google Summer of Code program (GSoC) encourages student participation in open source development. Accepted students are paired with OWASP mentors who guide implementation across real security-focused deliverables. OWASP BLT participates in GSoC through the OWASP Foundation, connecting students with real-world open-source security work since 2020.

This program runs completely online. Students and mentors from more than 100 countries have participated over past years.

Program Benefits

For Students

  • Gaining exposure to real-world software development scenarios
  • An opportunity for employment in areas related to their academic pursuits
  • Google offers successful student contributors a stipend
  • Build your professional network
  • Gain valuable experience in cybersecurity
  • Receive a certificate upon completion

For Mentors

  • Shape the next generation of open-source developers
  • Enhance project visibility and community engagement
  • Develop leadership and mentoring skills
  • Expand your professional network within OWASP
  • Recognition in the global security community
  • Contribute to the growth of open-source security projects
